About Us

Tokoza Youth Code Hub was started in 2023 in a small container with 5 laptops. We saw that many young people in Tokoza do not have access to computers and coding skills.

Our Mission

To provide free coding education to youth aged 14-24 in Tokoza.

Our Vision

A digitally empowered Tokoza where every youth can build a future in tech.
